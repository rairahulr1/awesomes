# awesome-ai-engineering

> A curated collection of AI engineering resources, roadmaps, tools, and projects covering LLMs,
> RAG, AI Agents, MCP, fine-tuning, evaluations, and production AI systems.

**Source:** [github.com/dipakkr/awesome-ai-engineering](https://github.com/dipakkr/awesome-ai-engineering)
· ★ 22.3k · MIT

The repo's About says AI engineering. The README says "A to Z Resources for Students" — it is a
general developer-resources mega-list that grew an AI section. That mismatch is the first thing to
know before using it.

## Structure

Main categories:

1. Programming Languages & Frameworks
2. Student Programs & Opportunities
3. Hackathons & Competitions
4. Learning Resources
5. Free Resources & Tools
6. Interview Preparation
7. Community & Networking
8. Additional Resources

Trending technologies (2025): Web3 & blockchain · AI & ML · Cybersecurity · Cloud & DevOps · Modern frontend

`IoT/`, `Robotics/`, `CheatSheet/`, `GSOC/`, `Intern Abroad/`, `Projects/`, `Archive.md`

## AI content worth knowing about

- **AI tools & resources** — ToolJunction directory, plus popular tools (ChatGPT, Midjourney, RunwayML, InVideo)
- **Machine learning** — CS229, Andrew Ng, Kaggle, research papers (ArXiv, Papers with Code, OpenReview)
- **LLMs** — Stanford CS25, DeepLearning.AI LLMOps w/ AWS, Hugging Face courses, LangChain short course, Cohere Academy, prompt engineering guides; playgrounds for GPT, Llama, Gemini, Claude, OpenRouter
- **Deep learning** — Andrew Ng's Deep Learning Specialisation, fast.ai, D2L, PyTorch and TensorFlow certificates, Top-Deep-Learning, DensePose
- **Web3 & blockchain** — platforms (Ethereum, Solana, Polygon, Avalanche, BSC, Cardano), learning (CryptoZombies, Buildspace, Alchemy University, OpenZeppelin, Web3 University), tooling (Hardhat, Truffle, Web3.js, Ethers.js, MetaMask, Remix, Foundry)
- **Cybersecurity** — TryHackMe, HackTheBox, Cybrary, SANS; certs (Security+, CEH, CISSP, OSCP, CISM); practice (OverTheWire, PicoCTF, VulnHub, HackThisSite, Root-Me); Kali, Burp, Nmap, Wireshark, Metasploit
- **Cloud & DevOps** — AWS/GCP/Azure/DO/Linode/Vultr; Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins, GitLab CI, Terraform, Ansible, Prometheus; AWS SA, GCP Pro, Azure Fundamentals, Docker Associate, CKA
- **Frontend** — Next.js, Svelte, Astro, SolidJS, Qwik, Remix; Tailwind, Chakra, Framer Motion, Headless UI, Mantine, Radix; Vite, Turbopack, Parcel, webpack, Rollup, esbuild; Zustand, Jotai, Valtio, Redux Toolkit, Recoil
- **Backend** — Django, Flask, Node.js/Express, with FastAPI, Rails, Spring Boot, Go, Rust

## Notes

- 22 GitHub topics for one repo — an unusually dense topical footprint, good for discovery.
- The "A to Z Resources for Students" origin means a large fraction of the content is
  beginner-oriented courses and roadmaps, not production AI engineering. Filter accordingly.
- "Trending Technologies (**2025**)" heading is dated relative to today. Treat the roadmap framing
  as stale even where individual links still resolve.
