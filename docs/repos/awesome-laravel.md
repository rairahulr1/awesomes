## Notes

- Requires Travis config on PRs — a 2015-era CI convention. Expect contribution friction.
- Local user groups and meetup sections are the most actively maintained part; the Laracon
  playlist list is clearly historical (last entry 2013).
- Roughly a fifth of the "Videos" section points at Udemy/Lynda/Pluralsight affiliate links.
  Treat as marketing inventory rather than neutral curation.
