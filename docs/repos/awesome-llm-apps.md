- **Advanced AI Agents** — production-style with tools, memory, multi-step reasoning
  - AI home renovation, DevPulse signal intelligence, Deep research agent
  - VC due-diligence team, Research planner/executor, System architect (DeepSeek R1 + Claude)
  - Financial coach, Movie production, Investment, Earnings-call analyst
  - Health & fitness, Product-launch intelligence, Fraud investigation, Journalist
  - Mental wellbeing, Meeting agent, Self-evolving agent (EvoAgentX)
  - Sales intelligence team, News & podcast, Openwork browser automation, Trust-gated research team
- **Always-on Agents** — scheduled, event-driven
  - Hacker News briefing (Slack/email), Release radar
- **Multi-agent Teams** — collaboration across domains
  - Competitor intelligence, Finance team, Game design team
  - AG2 adaptive research, Legal team, Recruitment team
  - Real estate, Services agency (CrewAI), Teaching team
  - Multimodal coding / design / UI-feedback teams

- **MCP AI agents** — Browser, GitHub, Notion, Travel planner (live Airbnb + Maps), multi-MCP router, OpenAI remote MCP bridge
- **RAG pipelines** — from minimal chains to agentic and multi-source
